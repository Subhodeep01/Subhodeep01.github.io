Research

Active threads in fairness, agentic systems, and responsible data

Work in Progress

Eight active projects spanning algorithmic fairness, agentic AI systems, and responsible data management. Several are under review; the remainder are ongoing in the Big Data Analytics Lab at NJIT.

View Published Work
01 Under review • KDD 2027

Reordering Stream Output for Group Fairness

Our block-level fairness framework allows for in-depth fairness enforcement in windows, thus increasing the effectiveness of reordering for larger windows. We propose optimal, efficient reordering algorithms that, upon detecting a fairness violation, will rearrange elements in the current window to maximize the number of fair blocks while maintaining the semantics of the stream.

Pre-print GitHub
02 Ongoing

Maximizing Submodular Functions Constrained to Multiple Fairness Attributes

We work towards building a set of candidates that not only satisfy the upper and approximate lower bounds of fairness but also maximize the output of a monotone submodular function, by using bipartite exchange graphs.

03 Ongoing

Local Intrinsic Dimensionality for Feature Selection

We use Local Intrinsic Dimensionality to score how much each feature locally contributes to the joint complexity of the data, and use this to select or prune features by comparing each feature's marginal LID against the joint LID. Features that add little to the joint LID relative to their own marginal LID are treated as locally redundant and dropped.

GitHub
04 Ongoing

LLM-Powered Best Set Recommendation

A general computational framework that supports arbitrary, user-defined set-based scoring functions over multi-modal data. We introduce a principled probabilistic model to estimate the likelihood of correctly identifying the top-k set given user preferences, and develop efficient algorithms to guide this selection process.

Pre-print GitHub
05 Ongoing

Large-Scale Fusion Knowledge Management with Agentic AI

Large-scale fusion experiments create huge and tightly correlated datasets, but maintaining plasma shot logbooks remains a manual task — a bottleneck for scaling and provenance. We propose an agent-based AI system for managing fusion knowledge at the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory, providing scalable provenance logging in a vector search database.

06 Ongoing

Rethinking Fairness in LLM Embeddings

Fair2Vec creates unbiased gender representations by incorporating the classification network within the Skip-gram training process — bias mitigation that cannot be achieved through post-training processes alone. We are also analyzing the inner workings of embedding spaces in large language models to neutralize socially entrenched biases.

GitHub
07 Ongoing

Robust and Non-Robust Features in Adversarial Scenarios

We study the adversarial robustness of gradient-based and tree-based models when trained on a spectrum of increasingly perturbed datasets that only retain robust and non-robust features. The findings shed light on how a model perceives an input, and we are looking at ways to incorporate them to create more robust models.

Initial Findings
08 Accepted • ACM CHI BiAlign 2026

Rationalize: Shared Semantic Reasoning for Human-AI Alignment

We conceptualize human-AI interaction as a series of complementary role pairs operating in a shared reasoning space. We relate these role pairs to the bidirectional human-AI alignment framework, illustrating how aligning AI to humans and aligning humans to AI differ by role, and sketch a collaborative research agenda for alignment design and assessment.
